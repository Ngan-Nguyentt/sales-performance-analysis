# Sales Performance Analysis

This project analyzes sales data in Power BI to track overall performance and break it down by product category, customer segment, geography, and time — turning raw orders into a clear, interactive performance story.

## Objectives & Key Metrics
- **Key Questions:** How are total sales, profit, and quantity trending? Which categories and customer segments perform best? How does performance vary by state and over time?
- **Key Metrics:** *Sum of Sales*, *Sum of Profit*, and *Sum of Quantity*, surfaced as headline KPI cards.
- **Dimensions:** **Category**, **Segment**, **State** (mapped geographically), and a full **Order Date** hierarchy (Year → Quarter → Month → Day).

## Key Findings
- **Headline KPIs:** Card visuals give an at-a-glance read on total Sales, Profit, and Quantity.
- **Mix analysis:** Pie and donut charts break performance down by category and segment, showing the contribution mix.
- **Geography:** An Azure Map plots performance by state, revealing strong and weak regions spatially.
- **Trend:** Combo and column charts track performance across the date hierarchy, supporting drill-down from year to day.

> *Tip: replace or extend the bullets above with the specific figures from your own analysis (e.g. "Consumer segment drives X% of sales").*

## Visuals & Documentation
- **Report file:** `sales-performance-analysis.pbix` — download and open in [Power BI Desktop](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/desktop/) to explore the full data model and visuals.
- **Charts used:** KPI cards, pie & donut charts, clustered column, line/stacked-column combo, and an Azure Map, with a slicer for interactive filtering.
